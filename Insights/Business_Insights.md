# 4. Business Recommendations
* Promote popular pizzas through targeted marketing campaigns.
* Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand pizza sizes.
* Introduce special offers during low-demand hours.
* Focus on high-revenue-generating pizza categories.
* Review low-performing products and consider improving their pricing or promotion.
* Use daily sales trends to improve staffing and inventory planning.

# 5. Final Conclusion
The Pizza Sales Analysis project demonstrates how SQL can transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. The analysis helps identify popular products, understand customer preferences, evaluate revenue performance, and discover ordering patterns.
